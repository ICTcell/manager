About

Kinnari Vijaykumar Mishra is an Assistant Professor in the Computer Engineering Department at PIET–DS, Parul University, and currently serves as Manager of the university's ICT Cell — a role in which she drives technology adoption, digital-learning initiatives, and effective use of institutional ICT platforms across campus.

"Versatile and goal-oriented — using my skills for the growth of the organization, and growing myself along with it."

Since joining Parul University in August 2019, she has taught across programming, databases, and data-driven systems, while steadily building a research portfolio in machine learning, data mining and text/emotion analysis. She is currently pursuing her PhD at Parul University, and has authored three published books on programming and data mining alongside nine peer-reviewed publications.

Career Summary — Fifteen-plus years spanning classroom teaching, curriculum delivery, faculty development, and institutional technology leadership: from early lecturing roles at Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Open University and Saurashtra University, through polytechnic and university teaching at Parul University, to her present dual role as Assistant Professor and ICT Cell Manager, where she trains faculty on AI research tools, LaTeX, LMS platforms and e-content development.
